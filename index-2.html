Our Story

Redefining The Sweet Treat Experience

CandyLoop started with a simple mission: take classic nostalgic treats and elevate them into modern culinary masterpieces. We don't just serve food; we serve an experience.

From spinning massive organic cotton candy clouds to frying decadent, Instagram-worthy loaded funnel cakes, every item is made fresh to order right before your eyes.
